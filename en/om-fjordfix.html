About FjordFix

Practical help for home and garden
– done the local way.

FjordFix is a local caretaker company offering practical help indoors and outdoors for private homeowners, landlords, housing associations, and small businesses.

FjordFix is run by me, Krister. I enjoy practical work and thrive when things are in order. I care about doing jobs properly, keeping things tidy along the way, and delivering a neat, clean result.

FjordFix was created to help with tasks that are often postponed but matter a lot for overall impression, function, and well‑being. It can be small jobs, maintenance, or steady follow‑up over time. Some need help once, others want a regular plan. Both are completely fine.

How I work

I focus on responsibility, clear communication, and tidy execution. When I take on a job, the customer should know what will be done and how it will be handled.

I do not take work that requires certified trades in fixed installations such as electrical work, plumbing, or wet rooms. If a task should be handled by specialists, I say so. It is important to be honest and give good advice, even when that means recommending others.

Wonderfully local

FjordFix is based at Kvammegardene just outside Knarvik. I take jobs in these municipalities:

  • 🔧 Alver municipality
  • 🔧 Bergen municipality
  • 🔧 Osterøy municipality

For larger or more extensive jobs, I may also be open to projects outside this area by agreement.

Businesses online

FjordFix also helps small and medium-sized businesses get online. Plenty of capable local tradespeople and small firms have no website at all, or one that has not been touched in years.

I build simple, fast websites that work on mobile, are easy to find in search, and that you do not have to think about afterwards. Same principle as everything else: a clear agreement, tidy execution, and something that still works once the job is delivered.

If your business deserves to be seen, get in touch for a no-obligation chat.

FjordFix is growing

The ship is currently steered by one person, which allows personal follow-up, flexibility, and adaptation to each customer. The ambition is to build the business further in a tidy and sustainable way, with a focus on quality and trust.

The goal is simple: to be a local and reliable provider people feel safe contacting, delivering practical help in a proper way.

If you have something that should have been done, whether once or regularly, feel free to get in touch. We can have a no‑obligation chat about what you need help with and find a solution that fits.
