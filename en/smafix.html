🛠️ HandyFix – practical help indoors and out

HandyFix is for people who need help with small practical tasks that often get postponed, but make a big difference once they are done.

These are jobs that are too small for a tradesperson, but too important to leave unfinished.

HandyFix is a good fit when you want to get one or more tasks done in an efficient and tidy way.

What can HandyFix be used for?
  • Mounting shelves, curtains, and TV brackets
  • Small repairs and adjustments
  • Replacing a faucet or mixer tap
  • Adjusting doors, drawers, and fittings
  • Simple indoor maintenance tasks
  • Applying new sealant
  • Pressure washing
  • Minor outdoor tasks such as tidying or dismantling
When is HandyFix a good fit?
  • When you have several small jobs that have been left unfinished
  • When you want to clear practical tasks in one session
  • When the task is too small to book a tradesperson
  • For older people or others who want practical help
  • Before a sale, rental, or visit
How FjordFix works
  • Jobs are agreed in advance
  • Clear pricing and expected time use
  • Efficient execution
  • A tidy finish

HandyFix is about getting things done without unnecessary hassle.
